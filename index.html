Runs in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

Every rep, read in three phases

  1. 1. Setup

    Lusion checks your back angle at the start, so you begin with your chest up.

  2. 2. Pull

    It measures hip and knee extension at the top of the pull, and how close the bar stays to your body.

  3. 3. Catch and stand

    It looks at catch depth and elbow lockout overhead, then shows your results.

Five checks after every rep

CheckWhat it looks atCue you might see
Setup positionBack angle from vertical at the startChest higher at the start.
ExtensionHip and knee angle at the top of the pullFinish the extension before pulling under.
Bar close to bodyWrist-to-ankle drift, in torso lengthsKeep the bar close to your body.
Catch depthHip height relative to the kneeSit deeper in the catch.
LockoutElbow angle overheadLock your elbows overhead.

Lusion does not track the bar. Your wrists stand in for it.
