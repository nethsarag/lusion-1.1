About Lusion AI

An all in one AI platform for weight lifters and coaches.

Watch every rep

Your webcam and a pose model read each snatch rep as it happens. Video stays in your browser.

Clear cues

After each rep you get a score and up to two short coaching cues you can act on.

Built for coaches too

Coaches follow progress, assign guides and keep lifters on track from one dashboard.

Lusion 1.1 supports the snatch only. More lifts are planned.

Lusion AI is an early MVP. Feedback is guidance, not a replacement for a qualified coach.
